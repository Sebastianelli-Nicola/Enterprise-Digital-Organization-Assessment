Comprehensive organizational and digital audit conducted for **Polo 9 Società Cooperativa Sociale** (Ancona, Italy). The study applies official Chamber of Commerce assessment tools (**ZOOM 4.0 Digital Maturity**, **UNI/PdR 134:2022 Sustainability Rating**, and **PID-CyberCheck Security Assessment**) to evaluate the organization's technological readiness, social/environmental impact, and cyber risk posture, proposing strategic roadmaps toward **Industry 4.0** and **Zero Trust** architectures.

Developed for the *Organizzazione dell'Impresa* course — M.Sc. in **Computer and Automation Engineering** at **Università Politecnica delle Marche (UNIVPM)** (A.Y. 2024/2025).

## Case Study Overview

* **Enterprise:** Polo 9 Società Cooperativa Sociale (Ancona, Marche, Italy)
* **Core Activity:** Social enterprise and training agency operating across the Marche region, managing 59 active projects (27 as lead partner), serving 1,355 beneficiaries, with 221 members and 345 employees (86% female) and an annual turnover exceeding €13 million.
* **Service Verticals:** Vocational training (accredited by Regione Marche), urban regeneration, educational services for children/youth, addiction recovery, equal opportunities, migrant integration, elderly care, and corporate welfare consulting.

## 1. ZOOM 4.0 Digital Maturity Assessment

The digital audit evaluated Polo 9 across eight key organizational dimensions using the official Chamber of Commerce PID 4.0 framework:

* **Overall Digital Predisposition Score: 3.00 / 5.00 (Advanced Level).**
  * *Interpretation:* The enterprise has adopted advanced technologies to effectively leverage digital opportunities. Internal processes show a high degree of automation, optimization, and structural orientation toward innovation.
* **Digital Dimensions Evaluation:**
  * *Digital Communication to Employees:* 4.5 / 5.0 (Intranet portals, collaboration tools).
  * *Digital Communication to Clients:* 4.0 / 5.0 (Online presence, social media, digital marketing).
  * *Business Innovation Predisposition:* 3.5 / 5.0 (R&D partnerships, advanced tech investments).
  * *Technological Infrastructure:* 3.75 / 5.0 (IT systems, data management).
  * *Staff Digital Readiness:* 4.1 / 5.0 (Management change support, soft/hard skills).
  * *Artificial Intelligence Adoption:* 4.5 / 5.0 (Integration and readiness for AI tools).
  * *Environmental Sustainability:* 1.25 / 5.0 (Identified as a major internal gap requiring structured digital tracking).
  * *Organizational & Social Sustainability:* 4.0 / 5.0 (Strategic alignment with social mission).
* **Human Resources & Skills Gap Analysis:**
  * *Current Competencies:* E-commerce, mobile payments, digital communication, data analysis, basic networking, IT security, and multidisciplinary teamwork.
  * *Target Competencies (Short-term):* Advanced IoT architectures, cloud infrastructure management, and specialized AI/Machine Learning models.

## 2. Corporate Sustainability & ESG Assessment

* **Sustainability Rating (UNI/PdR 134:2022):** Classified at the **"Avviato" (Started)** level (total affirmative answers placing it in the second tier of maturity), indicating established environmental policies and clear monitoring goals, but lacking a fully unified management system.
* **UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs):** High alignment with social and inclusion-oriented goals (SDG 3 Good Health, SDG 4 Quality Education, SDG 5 Gender Equality, SDG 8 Decent Work, SDG 10 Reduced Inequalities, SDG 16 Peace and Justice).
* **Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Compliance:**
  * *Social GRI:* 93% compatibility.
  * *Governance GRI:* 83% compatibility.

* **Process Management (PDCA Cycle):**
  * Planning (Plan): 48%

  * Measurement (Check/Act): 37% (indicating a critical need for continuous automated metric collection).

##  Strategic Improvement Proposals

### 1. Sustainable Management Integrated System (SIGS)
* **IoT Environmental Monitoring:** Deployment of IoT sensors across operating offices and classrooms to track energy consumption, indoor air quality, water usage, and waste generation in real time, projecting an estimated **15% energy savings** in the first year.
* **ESG Supply Chain Policy:** Prioritizing local, certified ESG suppliers for catering, office supplies, and furniture to reduce logistical carbon footprints.
* **Stakeholder Engagement & Gamification:** Establishing a "Sustainability Community" and a green-credit gamification system rewarding employees and beneficiaries for sustainable practices.

### 2. Industry 4.0 & Emerging Technologies (AI & Blockchain)
* **Predictive Facility Management & AI:** Smart building automation utilizing predictive maintenance algorithms to optimize HVAC and lighting based on real-time room occupancy.
* **Blockchain Certification (Hyperledger):** Implementing permissioned ledger technology for automated, immutable issuance and verification of professional training certificates, streamlining credential verification for regional accreditors and corporate partners.

### 3. Hybrid Cloud Infrastructure & Zero Trust Cybersecurity
* **Multi-Cloud & DRaaS:** Transitioning to an optimized hybrid cloud architecture with automated containerization (Infrastructure as Code) and Disaster Recovery as a Service (DRaaS) ensuring near-zero RPO and minimal RTO.
* **Zero Trust Security Paradigm:** Extending strict perimeter defense via continuous Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), network micro-segmentation, centralized SIEM/SOC monitoring, and TLS end-to-end encryption to comply with GDPR and ISO 27001 standards.
